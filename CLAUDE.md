# Required project guidance

Read and follow [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) before screen or animation work.
Read [the animation/device incident and validation guide](docs/ANIMATION_DEVICE_RELIABILITY.md)
for the known failure, approved motion policy, test commands and recording rules.

Do not treat automated-browser recordings as proof of behavior in the user's
existing local browser. Preserve the difference between automated, emulated and
physical-device verification in every completion report.
